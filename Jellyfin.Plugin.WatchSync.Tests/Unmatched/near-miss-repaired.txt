# The repair of the near-miss next to it, and nothing else. One character comes off
# one heading. If the guard passes this and refuses its neighbour, what it refused
# was the mistake and not something else about the fixture.

### `NoIdentifierAtAll`

The item carries no provider identifier of any kind.

### `NoEpisodeNumber`

The episode carries no episode number.

### `Ambiguous`

More than one local item claims the key.

| reason | effect | what the repair is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NoIdentifierAtAll` | refresh | An identifier on the item. |
| `NoEpisodeNumber` | refresh | The episode number. |
| `Ambiguous` | sweep | The duplicate removed. |
