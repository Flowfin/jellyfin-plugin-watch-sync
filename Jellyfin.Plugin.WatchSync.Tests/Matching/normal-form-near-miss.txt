A fixture standing in for the normal form table in docs/matching.md.

It carries its own vocabulary. A fixture judged against the real table would prove
the state of the tree on the day it was written rather than proving the guard.

One provider name is misspelled by one character. Everything else about the row is
right: it is in the right place, it has the right number of columns, and its normal
form and its refusals are the ones that provider actually has. That is the mistake a
hand maintained table produces, and it is invisible to a reader who is checking the
prose rather than the spelling.

| provider | normal form | also accepted, and normalised to it | refused as unusable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Imdb` | `tt` in lower case, then the digits with leading zeros removed and then padded back out to seven | the prefix in any case or absent altogether, and any amount of zero padding | a digit run shorter than seven |
| `Tmbd` | the digits with leading zeros removed | any amount of leading zero padding | a value carrying anything that is not a digit |
| `Tvdb` | the digits with leading zeros removed | any amount of leading zero padding | a value carrying anything that is not a digit |
