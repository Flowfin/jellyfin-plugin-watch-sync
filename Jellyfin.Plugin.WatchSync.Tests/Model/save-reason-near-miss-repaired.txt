# The repair of save-reason-near-miss.txt, and the repair is the one character the
# near-miss dropped. Nothing else about the table differs, so a guard that passes
# this and refuses the other is refusing the misspelling and not something else
# about the fixture.

- The treatment `enqueued` turns a moved field that differs from the agreed value
  into one change.
- The treatment `dropped` counts the event at the handler and carries it no further.

| the reason | treatment | what the server writes under it | why that treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PlaybackStart` | enqueued | `PlayCount` | The server has counted a play. |
| `PlaybackFinished` | enqueued | `PlaybackPositionTicks` | The stop is the position worth carrying. |
| `UpdateUserRating` | dropped | `IsFavorite` | No moved field can change under it. |
