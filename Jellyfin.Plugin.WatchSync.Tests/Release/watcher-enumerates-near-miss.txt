A slice of the watcher's sweep with the set it examines narrowed to the one workflow the
issue is titled for. It is the shape a reader arrives at on their own: the issue is about a
publish that ships nothing, so watching the publish reads as precision rather than as a
list. What it costs is the seventh workflow. The board this is modelled on watched six by
name and the one that went red for fifteen hours was not among them.

          jq -r --arg self "$SELF" --arg cutoff "$cutoff" --arg branch "$branch" '
            .[]
            | select(.workflowName != $self)
            | select(.workflowName == "Publish Release")
            | select(.updatedAt >= $cutoff)
            | select((.conclusion // "") as $c
                     | $c != "success" and $c != "skipped" and $c != "cancelled" and $c != "")
            | [.url, .conclusion, .event, .workflowName] | @tsv
          ' runs.json > red.tsv
