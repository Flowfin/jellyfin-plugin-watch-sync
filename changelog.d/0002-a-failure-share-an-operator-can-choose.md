The configuration page offers one more setting: how much of one exchange may fail before it
stops, in per cent. It keeps the value it had before, so a server that is upgraded and left
alone behaves exactly as it did.

Lowering it is the direction to be careful about. Near the floor, a single film that has been
deleted from a library stops every exchange, which is the outcome the rule exists to prevent
reached through the rule itself. That is why the page refuses anything below a quarter.
